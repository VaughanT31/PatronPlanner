# Shopping list

*Everything your queued orders still need, counted once, with prices.*

The shopping list adds up what the queued orders still need, counted once across the queue, with what you already have and what the rest costs.

- Open it anywhere, the Auction House included: `/patronplanner shop` or the minimap button.
- **With Auction Coach:** one click adds the list to Auction Coach's shopping list, which tells you when each item is listed cheaply.
- **With Auctionator:** one click makes an Auctionator shopping list.

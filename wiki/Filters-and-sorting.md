# Filters and sorting

*Find the orders worth doing in one click.*

The **summary bar** above the cards shows how many orders there are, how many you can craft, the total profit on offer and how much concentration you have.

### Filters

Each filter shows how many orders it matches:

- **All**
- **Can craft**
- **Profitable**
- **No concentration**
- **Nothing to buy**: orders you can fill from the materials you own (bags, banks and warband bank)
- **Bonus rewards**: orders with knowledge and other extra rewards
- **Unlearned**

### Sorting

Sort by **Best**, **Profit**, **Per concentration** (gold for each point of concentration, so you spend it where it pays most), **Reward** or **Time left**.

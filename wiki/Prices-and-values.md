# Prices and values

*Where the gold numbers come from, and how to value concentration and Moxie.*

Material costs and reward values come from whichever price addon you have: **Auction Coach**, **TradeSkillMaster** or **Auctionator**. Reward items count at what they sell for after the Auction House cut.

Without a price addon, the cards still show quality reach, time left and the gold reward, just without costs and profit.

### Values

The **Values** button lets you say what things without a price are worth to you:

- **Concentration:** what a point is worth. It's taken off the profit of orders that use it.
- **Moxie:** added to the reward. There's an estimate from what a Master's Surplus bag holds.
- **Knowledge and other rewards.**

# Knowledge

*What each profession can still earn this week, and every one-time treasure.*

Click **Knowledge** above the cards, type `/patronplanner knowledge`, or middle-click the minimap button.

### This week

For every Midnight crafting and gathering profession on your character:

- the **Thalassian Treatise**
- your **trainer's weekly quest**
- the **weekly treasure drops**
- **gathering and disenchanting drops**, with how many you've had
- the **Darkmoon Faire** quest while the Faire is on

It shows the points left this week, and marks anything you've looted but not used yet, so no knowledge sits forgotten in your bags.

Tick **Hide completed** to show only what's still to do. Anything waiting in your bags stays listed until you use it.

Click a profession's name to fold it away, and again to open it. The window grows and shrinks to fit.

Click the **lock** beside the close button to pin the window. Pinned, it stays open through reloads, logins and loading screens, and Escape leaves it open. Closing it unpins it. It remembers where you put it either way.

Used a treatise or notepad and it isn't ticked? **Right-click** its line to mark it done for this week. Right-click again to undo.

> For crafting professions, the trainer's weekly quest is the *Services Requested* quest from Captain Flaresworn in Silvermoon. It gives a notepad or journal, and the knowledge counts once you use it.

### Treasures

All 8 one-time treasures per profession, with the zone and coordinates. Click one for a waypoint: TomTom when it's installed, otherwise a map pin.

### Your points

Your unspent knowledge, the **catch-up** knowledge still available (it comes from patron orders when you're behind), about how much finishes the tree, and an **All characters** view with every character's points left.

# Questions and fixes

*Answers to the things people ask most.*

**There are no gold or profit numbers.**

Patron Planner needs a price addon for those: Auction Coach, TradeSkillMaster or Auctionator. Without one, the cards still show quality reach, time left and the gold reward.

**Profit has an asterisk (*) next to it.**

Some items in that order had no price yet, so the real profit may be a little higher or lower.

**Public orders show Blizzard's list, not cards.**

Public orders grouped by item keep Blizzard's view. Pick an item and its orders show as cards.

**`/pp` opens Push Planner.**

Both addons use `/pp`. Use `/patronplanner` instead.

**Craft says I'm short of materials or not at my table.**

Craft uses the materials planned for the order. Buy what the shopping list shows, and stand at your profession's crafting table.

**Which game versions does it support?**

Retail (Midnight) only.

**How do I go back to Blizzard's list?**

Click the button beside the order tabs, or type `/patronplanner classic`.

# Patron Planner wiki

How to use Patron Planner: reading the order cards, finding the orders worth doing, working through them from the queue, the shopping list and the Knowledge window.

Also on the website: [Patron Planner wiki](https://ctrlshiftzed.com/wiki/patron-planner/) | Get it on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/patron-planner-better-view-of-patron-orders)

- [Reading an order card](Reading-an-order-card.md): What each part of a card means, and what the coloured edge tells you.
- [Filters and sorting](Filters-and-sorting.md): Find the orders worth doing in one click.
- [The order queue](The-order-queue.md): Queue the orders you want, then claim, craft and hand them in from one panel.
- [Shopping list](Shopping-list.md): Everything your queued orders still need, counted once, with prices.
- [Knowledge](Knowledge.md): What each profession can still earn this week, and every one-time treasure.
- [Prices and values](Prices-and-values.md): Where the gold numbers come from, and how to value concentration and Moxie.
- [Commands](Commands.md): Every Patron Planner chat command.
- [Questions and fixes](Questions-and-fixes.md): Answers to the things people ask most.

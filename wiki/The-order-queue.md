# The order queue

*Queue the orders you want, then claim, craft and hand them in from one panel.*

### Queue orders

- Press **+** on a card, or right-click it, to queue it.
- **Queue all** adds every profitable order on the board. Chat says why any were left out.

### Work through the queue

The queue sits beside the profession window with a button per order:

1. **Claim** the order.
2. **Craft** it. Patron Planner uses the planned materials and concentration, checks the quality just before crafting, and tells you if you're short of materials or away from your crafting table.
3. **Hand in.**

**Next** does the next step for you, so a whole queue goes by one button.

Show or hide the queue with `/patronplanner queue` or the minimap button.

# The order queue

*Queue the orders you want, then claim, craft and hand them in from one panel.*

### Queue orders

- Press **+** on a card, or right-click it, to queue it.
- **Queue all** adds every profitable order on the board. Chat says why any were left out.

Queued orders take the materials you own first, in queue order, so the same stack never pays for two orders. Every other card then shows what's left, and its tooltip says how many are kept for your queue.

### Work through the queue

The queue sits beside the profession window with a button per order:

1. **Claim** the order.
2. **Craft** it. Patron Planner uses the planned materials (the cheapest mix of qualities that reaches the order) and concentration, checks the quality just before crafting, and tells you if you're short of materials or away from your crafting table.
3. **Hand in.**

**Next** does the next step for you, so a whole queue goes by one button.

Show or hide the queue with `/patronplanner queue` or the minimap button.

# Getting started

*Install it, open Crafting Orders, pick a tab, and the cards are there.*

Patron Planner turns the Crafting Orders tabs into cards that show, for every order, what your materials cost, what the reward is worth, the profit, and whether you can reach the quality asked for.

### Install

1. **CurseForge app:** search for **Patron Planner** and click Install.
2. **By hand:** download it from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/patron-planner-better-view-of-patron-orders) and put the folder in `World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns`.
3. Start the game, or type `/reload`.

### First use

1. Go to a **Crafting Orders** table in your profession's city.
2. Pick a tab: **Patron**, **Personal**, **Guild** or **Public**.
3. The orders show as cards. See [Reading an order card](Reading-an-order-card.md).

There's no setup. For the gold columns you need a price addon: Auction Coach, TradeSkillMaster or Auctionator. See [Prices and values](Prices-and-values.md).

# Reading an order card

*What each part of a card means, and what the coloured edge tells you.*

Each card shows:

- **The item** and the **profit** in large print.
- **The quality the customer asked for**, and how you can reach it: with the cheapest materials, with materials you own, with concentration, or not yet.
- **Time left**: amber under 6 hours, red under 2.
- **The materials you supply**, with their quality and cost.
- **The reward**: gold and any items.

### The coloured edge

| Edge | Means |
|---|---|
| **Green** | A good order |
| **Amber** | Needs concentration or better materials |
| **Red** | A loss, or a quality you can't reach |
| **Grey** | A recipe you don't know (these sit at the bottom, with how to learn them) |

The **best pick**, the most profitable order you can actually craft, is marked for you.

### The full breakdown

Hover a card to see where the numbers come from: each reward item and its value, each material and its cost, the quality needed and how much concentration it takes against what you have. Hold **Shift** for the item's own tooltip. Click the card to open the order as usual.

> Profit marked with an asterisk (*) means some items had no price yet, so the real number may be a little higher or lower.

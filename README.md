# Patron Planner

A World of Warcraft addon (Midnight, retail) that shows which patron crafting orders are worth doing, right on the Patron tab: what the materials you supply will cost, what the reward is worth, the profit, and whether you can reach the requested quality with cheap materials, the materials you own, or only with concentration.

Prices come from whichever of these you have installed: Auction Coach, TradeSkillMaster or Auctionator. It works without them too, just without the gold columns.

Get it on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/patron-planner-better-view-of-patron-orders). Bugs and ideas: [Discord](https://discord.gg/wz7fw6jM9Z).
